The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. Every tenant on file is day first, so every tenant is hit, through the app, email and the booking-v2 form. Day and month swap exactly; the weekday follows the wrong date.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. At a day-first tenant on booking-v3, people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The result looks like the parser bug, but only through that form, and fixing the parser does not touch it: the form needs the tenant’s date order in its hint (or a date picker).

shift: some dates move by one day. These are not swaps. Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May; a meeting reads Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before the booking was made. That is a time-zone or midnight-UTC bug. The desk already noted these as a one-day move, then filed them under the parser by tenant, where they would stay open after the parser fix.

How each report was placed

  1. Count each report id once.
  2. Read as a one-day move (desk notes): shift.
  3. Otherwise, by tenant (#lib/kb/causes): a day-first tenant’s report through booking-v3’s form is hint; any other report from a day-first tenant is parser.
  4. No tenant on file: unplaced, and someone reads it.
